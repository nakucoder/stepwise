Stepwise

Contents

Eight sections, in the order most people learn them

Each one has a worked example you can step through, a trace table filled in as it runs, and room for your own numbers.

1

Sorting

Put things in order, from bubble sort to quick sort.

Bubble, selection, insertion, merge, quick, heap, counting
2

Searching

Find one item fast by ruling out half the list each time.

Linear, binary, jump, interpolation
3

Linked lists

Chains of nodes, each one pointing to the next.

Insert, delete, reverse, find the middle, detect a loop
4

Trees

Branching data: search trees, heaps and traversals.

BST insert and delete, BFS, pre/in/post-order, heaps
5

Graphs

Maps and networks: shortest paths, BFS and DFS.

BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, A*, topological sort, Prim, Kruskal
6

Hashing

Jump straight to the right bucket instead of searching.

Chaining, linear probing, quadratic probing, resizing
7

Pattern matching

Find a word inside a long text without rereading it.

Brute force, KMP, Boyer-Moore
8

Dynamic programming

Solve each small piece once, save it, and reuse it.

Fibonacci, longest common subsequence, knapsack
