Stepwise
Level

Eight topics, in the order most people learn them

Each one has worked examples you can step through with your own numbers, a trace table that fills in as it runs, and the code for every step.

Pick a topic, type in your own numbers, and watch what happens one step at a time.

1

Put things in order, from bubble sort to quick sort.

Put a jumble of numbers in order, smallest to biggest.

Bubble, selection, insertion, merge, quick, heap, counting7 to try

2

Find the number you want without checking every one.

Linear, binary, jump, interpolation4 to try

3

Chains of nodes, each one pointing to the next.

A chain where each link knows where the next one is.
